Biography

Luke Amato

Somerville, NJ

I want to be...

Traveling the world

I'm 19 years old and a freshman at RVCC. I am currently studying Mechanical Engineering and hope to transfer to Rutgers my junior year. I was a CompSci major but just recently switched after I fell in love with the creative aspect of engineering. I hope one day to be working for defense contractors or in the construction area. It's such a broad field so my ideas are bound to change, but for now that's my plan.

Hobbies

  • Fishing

    Fishing is a big hobby of mine, I find it extremely relaxing and I love to be outdoors with nature. I go with my friends around the woods areas and catch fish. I've only been fishing for about a year, but it is some of the most fun I've ever had surprisingly.

  • Snowboarding

    I ride seasonally at resorts like Blue Mountain in the Poconos. Since the winter days don't allow much time to be outdoors, this is one of the best things I can still do. It is easily the most exhilarating experience I've ever had, and the adrenaline rush is a very addicting drug.

  • Guitar

    I've been playing for several years with a focus on blues playing. I love guitarists like Gary Moore, John Mayer, and my all time favorite, Jimi Hendrix. My guitar teacher is a family friend who's played in bands with my uncles and cousins.

  • Reading

    I enjoy reading across a range of genres like fiction and nonfiction, my most recent read was 1984 by George Orwell. It's a dystopian novel that focuses on a government that no one can stand up to. Another personal favorite read is The Hike by Drew Magary. It's about a man who gets lost on a trek, and gets sent through an Alice in Wonderland-style adventure.

  • 3D Modeling

    I've been utilizing 3D modeling software to create a large resume for my mechanical engineering projects. I've created products for the guitar, and remote controls.

  • Exercise

    Strength training is a regular part of my routine, I go to the gym 3-4 times each week and have been since my freshman year of High School. I hate leg day but it's necessary.

  • Travel

    Traveling is something I'm passionate about, I want to see as much of the world as I possibly can. My friend PJ and I are traveling to Italy in 3 weeks and staying there for 2 weeks. We are going from Milan, to Lake Como, to Florence, and ending off in Positano.

The Caterpillar and Alice looked at each other for some time in silence: at last the Caterpillar took the hookah out of its mouth, and addressed her in a languid, sleepy voice. 'Who are you?' said the Caterpillar. This was not an encouraging opening for a conversation. Alice replied, rather shyly, 'I—I hardly know, sir, just at present—at least I know who I was when I got up this morning, but I think I must have been changed several times since then.'

Alice was beginning to get very tired of sitting by her sister on the bank, and of having nothing to do: once or twice she had peeped into the book her sister was reading, but it had no pictures or conversations in it, 'and what is the use of a book,' thought Alice 'without pictures or conversation?'

The rabbit-hole went straight on like a tunnel for some way, and then dipped suddenly down, so suddenly that Alice had not a moment to think about stopping herself before she found herself falling down a very deep well. Either the well was very deep, or she fell very slowly, for she had plenty of time as she went down to look about her and to wonder what was going to happen next.

Alice opened the door and found that it led into a small passage, not much larger than a rat-hole: she knelt down and looked along the passage into the loveliest garden you ever saw.
